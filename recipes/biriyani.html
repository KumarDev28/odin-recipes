Chicken Biriyani

A fragrant and flavorful rice dish made with tender chicken, aromatic spices and basmati rice.

Ingredients

For the chicken

  • Chicken- 1kg
  • Yogurt- 1 cup
  • Onion- 3 large
  • Ginger-garlic paste- 2 tbsp
  • Biriyani masala- 2 tbsp
  • Salt- to taste

For the rice

  • Basmati rice- 500gm
  • Water- as needed
  • Cinnamon
  • Cardamon

Instructions

1. Marinate the chicken

Mix chicken with yogurt, spices, ginger-garlic paste and salt. Let it rest for at least 30 minutes.

2. Prepare the rice

Boil the basmati rice with whole spices until it is about 70% cooked.

3.Cook the chicken

Cook the marinated chicken until tender and flavorful.

4.Layer the biriyani

Add rice over the chicken and layer with fried onions and herbs.

4.Dum cook

Cover and cook on low heat until the rice ris completely cooked and aromatic
